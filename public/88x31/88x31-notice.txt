I am hosting these buttons myself for a few reasons:

Some sites have hotlinking blocked, including mine. I'm not 100% on proper web
etiquette, but as I understand it, hotlinking uses up someone else's resources
when you load an asset from their site. Depending on how they're hosting, this
may cost them money and/or slow down their site.

Additionally, while unlikely, they could change their button to something else
that's bad and I wouldn't know unless I happened to look or more likely, 
someone else tells me after seeing it first. If I host a copy of the button
then this can't happen.

For those reasons, I choose to host other's 88x31 myself. 88x31 buttons are 
meant to be shared anyways, so I figure this is fine, but if you'd really
like me to hotlink your button, please contact me. My socials are listed on
most pages and I will probably put an email in somewhere as well, I just
haven't gotten around to that at the time of writing this.
